Our Story

BILLY VAN CREAMY was founded in 2014 by brothers Mitch and Alex Wells. Starting with just one truck atop Ruckers Hill, Northcote, we now have stores in Melbourne and Perth. While the business continues to grow, our ingredients-driven approach to ice cream remains the same.

We strive to source all of our ingredients from smaller, local producers who share our commitment to wholesome, sustainably made food that caters to all.
